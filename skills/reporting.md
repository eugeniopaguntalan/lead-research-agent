# Reporting Skill

## Purpose

This document defines the reporting standards for the Lead Generation Agent project.

The purpose of reporting is to provide clear, actionable insights that help identify and prioritize potential customers.

Reports should help a human quickly answer:

* Which businesses should I contact?
* Why are they good prospects?
* What problems can my product solve for them?
* Which leads should be prioritized?

Reports must be concise, factual, and actionable.

---

# Core Principles

Reports must:

* Prioritize clarity
* Prioritize actionable information
* Highlight opportunities
* Explain reasoning
* Avoid unnecessary detail

Reports should not:

* Dump raw data
* Repeat information unnecessarily
* Include unsupported conclusions
* Include fabricated information

---

# Report Types

The system may generate multiple report types.

## Daily Lead Report

Generated daily.

Purpose:

Identify newly discovered leads and rank opportunities.

Audience:

Founder
Sales Representative
Business Development

---

## Weekly Summary Report

Generated weekly.

Purpose:

Summarize trends and opportunities discovered during the week.

Audience:

Founder
Management

---

## Opportunity Report

Generated on demand.

Purpose:

Provide detailed analysis of a specific business or industry.

Audience:

Founder
Sales Team

---

# Daily Lead Report Format

Reports must follow this structure.

```text
Daily Lead Report

Date

Summary

Top Opportunities

Lead Details

Recommendations

Next Actions
```

---

# Executive Summary

Always start with a short summary.

Include:

* Number of businesses analyzed
* Number of qualified opportunities
* Top industry categories
* Highest scoring lead

Example:

```text
Summary

50 businesses analyzed.

18 qualified opportunities identified.

Top industries:

- Veterinary Clinics
- Auto Repair Shops
- Medical Clinics

Highest scoring lead:

ABC Veterinary Clinic (Score: 92)
```

---

# Top Opportunities Section

Show the highest priority leads first.

Sort by:

1. Fit Score Descending
2. Opportunity Quality Descending

Maximum:

```text
Top 10 Leads
```

for daily reports.

---

# Lead Detail Format

Each lead should follow this format.

```text
Business Name

Industry

Location

Website

Fit Score

Why This Business Is A Good Fit

Recommended Features

Potential Risks

Analysis Summary
```

---

Example

```text
ABC Veterinary Clinic

Industry:
Veterinary Clinic

Location:
Melbourne, Australia

Website:
https://example.com

Fit Score:
92

Why This Business Is A Good Fit

- Walk-in consultations available
- Multiple veterinarians
- Reception desk present
- High customer waiting likelihood

Recommended Features

- Queue Tracking
- SMS Notifications
- Public Queue Display

Potential Risks

- Existing appointment software may already be in use

Analysis Summary

Strong opportunity due to high customer volume,
multiple service providers, and visible waiting process.
```


---

# Industry Breakdown

Show lead distribution.

Example:

```text
Industry Breakdown

Veterinary Clinics:
22

Medical Clinics:
18

Auto Repair Shops:
12

Car Washes:
8
```

---

# Market Insights

Identify patterns discovered during the week.

Example:

```text
Most veterinary clinics advertise walk-in services.

Many auto repair businesses do not provide queue visibility.

Several clinics rely on appointment systems but still experience waiting room congestion.
```

Insights must be evidence-based.

# Data Accuracy Requirements

Reports must only contain:

* Verified information
* Supported conclusions
* Traceable reasoning

Reports must never contain:

* Fabricated data
* Invented business information
* Unsupported assumptions

---

# Source Attribution

Whenever practical, include:

```text
Website
Source URL
```

for all business records.

This allows manual verification.

---

# Formatting Rules

Use Markdown.

Use:

```text
Headings
Bullets
Tables
```

when appropriate.

Avoid:

* Large paragraphs
* Walls of text
* Excessive formatting

Reports should be easy to scan in under five minutes.

# Quality Standards

Every report must:

* Be easy to read
* Explain reasoning
* Highlight opportunities
* Be actionable
* Be evidence-based
* Avoid speculation

A successful report enables a human to decide who to contact next without needing to review raw research data.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful report:

* Identifies the highest-value opportunities
* Clearly explains why opportunities were selected
* Provides actionable recommendations
* Minimizes noise
* Uses consistent formatting
* Enables faster sales decisions

The purpose of reporting is not to display data.

The purpose of reporting is to help make better business decisions.
